003 / Process

Tips for acing the interview/

01
# First things first.
Apply for a job or just send us your CV. Our HR managers will contact you shortly.
Tip: If you attach a cover letter, your chances of landing an interview rise significantly!
02
# Tech interview with the team
Look at the job offer once again and prepare questions before the interview. Refresh your knowledge about our tech stack. We hope to see your understanding and expertise.
Tip: It's more valuable to see an example of a theoretical issue put into practice rather than definitions.
03
# Final round with managers & CTO
The final interview is to get to know you better, see whether you and the team would make a perfect match, and discover your strong points. We'll discuss working conditions and future tasks.
Tip: Just relax and be yourself! We value honesty and open-mindedness.
